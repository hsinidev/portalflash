What is PortalFlash?


PortalFlash is a browser-based puzzle game developed by Valve Corporation, the same studio behind the popular Half-Life series. Released in 2011, this game challenges players to navigate through increasingly complex levels using portal technology to reach the exit.


Game Overview


In PortalFlash, players take on the role of an test subject who has been transported to a mysterious facility where they must use the portals to escape. The game features a unique blend of physics-based puzzle-solving and dark humor. Players will need to carefully plan their movements using the portal buttons to avoid deadly obstacles and reach the exit.


How to Play


To play PortalFlash, follow these basic steps:
• Click on each portal button to move your test subject to a new location.
• Use the left mouse button to rotate the portal buttons.
• Use the right mouse button to teleport yourself or your test subject to a specific location.
• Be careful not to get stuck in a loop of portals, as this can result in game over.
